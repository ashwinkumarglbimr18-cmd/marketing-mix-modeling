DISCLOSURE
----------
This dataset is SYNTHETIC. No real brand or transaction data is used.
It is modelled on the structure of Meta's Robyn simulated MMM dataset
(industry-standard for teaching MMM) and adapted to an India e-commerce
context with INR currency and Indian festival flags. The ground-truth
ROAS, adstock decay, and Hill saturation values listed below were used
to GENERATE the weekly_revenue series. Task 2 will fit a fresh model
to this dataset and we will compare recovered ROAS vs these ground-truth
numbers as a sanity check on the modelling pipeline.

CONTROL VARIABLES
-----------------
  is_diwali_week         : 1 for last 2 weeks of Oct + first week of Nov
  is_eid_week            : 1 for Eid al-Fitr week (Apr 2024, Mar 2025)
  is_republic_day_week   : 1 for last week of Jan each year
  is_eors_week           : 1 for 2 weeks in Jul + 2 weeks in Dec each year
  competitor_price_index : 90-110, sinusoidal + noise
  avg_product_price      : Rs 1,800 - Rs 2,400, slow oscillation + noise

REVENUE EQUATION (used to generate the target)
----------------------------------------------
  baseline               = Rs 35,00,000 weekly
  trend                  = linear 0 to Rs 2.5L over 104 weeks
  price_effect           = -200 * (avg_product_price - 2,100)
  competitor_effect      = -8,000 * (competitor_price_index - 100)
  channel_contributions  = sum across channels of:
                              beta_ch * adstock(spend, lambda)^alpha
                            where beta_ch is calibrated so average revenue-
                            per-rupee = target ROAS
  festival_lift          = +28L Diwali, +17.5L EORS,
                           +10.5L Eid, +10.5L Republic Day (additive in INR)
  noise                  = Gaussian, std = 5% of baseline

  weekly_revenue = baseline + trend + price_effect + competitor_effect
                   + channel_contributions + festival_lift + noise

HOW TO USE
----------
- Treat this dataset like a real MMM weekly file: regress weekly_revenue
  on adstocked + saturated channel spend plus the controls.
- After fitting, compare recovered ROAS per channel against the
  ground-truth ROAS above. Errors of +/- 25% are typical for MMM
  pipelines; errors over 50% suggest a modelling bug or insufficient
  data variability.

LIMITATIONS
-----------
- This is synthetic data generated by a fixed random seed (20260427)
  for full reproducibility.
- Real MMM data has additional sources of confounding (creative,
  competitor activity, macro shocks, brand-equity drift) that are not
  modelled here. Treat recovered ROAS as a proof-of-pipeline, not as
  business advice.
